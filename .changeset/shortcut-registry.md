A shortcut registry (enterprise UX study, roadmap #1): `ShortcutProvider`, `useShortcut`, `ShortcutScope`, `useShortcuts`, `useShortcutPreferences`, `useShortcutKeys` and the `?` sheet `ShortcutSheet`. One registry owns every key on the page: global and focus-scoped shortcuts (the innermost scope wins), sequences such as G then I with a timeout, `Mod` as ⌘ or Ctrl per platform, layout-aware matching (Cyrillic layouts, AltGr, Option), nothing while typing in a field, a contenteditable or a combobox, while an IME composes, on repeat, or under a modal dialog outside the scope; development warnings and `onConflict` for two shortcuts with the same keys in one scope (`findShortcutConflicts` for a static map); a switch that turns single-key shortcuts off and remaps by command id (WCAG 2.1.4, G217), stored like the display preferences under `uix-shortcut-preferences`. Hints read their keys from it: `KbdCombo`, `Button`, `ButtonLink`, `Tooltip`, `MenuItem` and the checkbox and radio rows take `shortcutId`, and `CommandPalette` items take `shortcutId` (keys, `aria-keyshortcuts`, and choosing the item runs the command). `KbdCombo` shows sequences ("G then I"). `CommandPalette` `shortcut` opens it through the registry. The kit's own keys now go through it with the same defaults: `AppShell` `navHotkey` (now also off with the single-key switch), `DataWorkspace` Ctrl/⌘+/ (Ctrl+/ on a Mac no longer focuses the search; ⌘/ does). New stylesheet `components/shortcut-sheet.css` and `.uix-kbd-combo__then` in kbd.css. Spec: `Docs/specs/2026-11-20-shortcut-registry.md`.
