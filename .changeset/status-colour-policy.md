**The status colour policy** (HAR-1564, colour K3 of the 2026-10-07 operator review): docs → Foundations → Status colour (`explorer.html#status-colour`). Neutral is the default for every lifecycle state; blue is in flight, green a positive outcome worth noticing, amber attention soon, red failed / breached / blocked and actionable now; a solid fill only for SEV-1. Treatment by surface (table `dot`, header `soft`, flag `outline`), a colour budget (one filled hued pill per row, three hues per screen, filled hues on at most 30% of rows), trends by sentiment, and the dark-mode targets. With a mapping matrix and the same ITSM queue rendered both ways. The Status pill, Alert, Stat tile, Scheduling calendar and Scheduling timeline pages link to it; `Docs/design-system.md` has the summary. Documentation only.
