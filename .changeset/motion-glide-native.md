Motion, rebuilt natively from a study of animate-ui (operator decisions 2026-11-08). No animate-ui code and no animation library: springs are CSS `linear()` curves on UIx tokens, and the rest is CSS plus small React hooks.

- **Tokens:** `--uix-ease-glide` / `--uix-dur-glide` (a spring with no overshoot, 280 ms, reads as ~175 ms) for marks that move between items; `--uix-ease-pop` / `--uix-dur-pop` (a 3 % overshoot) for a control growing from its trigger; `--uix-ease-tooltip` / `--uix-dur-tooltip` (animate-ui's tooltip spring, stiffness 400 / damping 31: 2 % overshoot, reads as ~165 ms, settled at 410 ms); `--uix-ease-enter` / `--uix-ease-leave` and `--uix-dur-swap-in` (340 ms) / `--uix-dur-swap-out` (130 ms) for content swapping inside a surface that stays; `--uix-swap-shift` (40 px); `--uix-swap-blur` (6 px) and `--uix-arrive-blur` (4 px); `--uix-ease-soft`, `--uix-ease-fill`, `--uix-ease-drawer`, `--uix-ease-fade`, `--uix-ease-bounce` / `--uix-dur-bounce` and the durations `--uix-dur-menu` (220 ms), `--uix-dur-fade-in` (100 ms), `--uix-dur-row` (300 ms), `--uix-dur-fade` (200 ms), `--uix-dur-draw` / `--uix-delay-draw`, `--uix-dur-overlay` (400 ms), `--uix-dur-drawer` / `--uix-dur-drawer-out`, `--uix-dur-disclosure` (350 ms), `--uix-hold-pick` (250 ms) and `--uix-dur-icon*` (animate-ui's curves and timings); `--uix-stagger-rows` (33 ms); `--uix-scrim-blur` (4 px). `--uix-ease-spring` stays for compatibility.
- **One gliding hover mark** in Sidebar, Menu and Select lists (`useHoverGlide`): a single `aria-hidden` `.uix-glide` layer moved to the row with `transform` alone (it never touches the list's layout), reaching rows of another size by `scale` (animate-ui's highlight); it fades in and out over 200 ms. Mouse, pen and the keyboard: a row reached with the arrow keys or Tab takes it too (operator, 2026-11-08). **Markup:** the sidebar `<nav>`, the menu popover and the select listbox gain an `aria-hidden` `.uix-glide` span as their first child; the sidebar also gains a hidden `.uix-sidebar__mark`. Exact-markup snapshots of these components need updating.
- **Sidebar:** the current-page tint slides to a clicked row (as Tabs); a group's rows arrive staggered from the inline end, clearing from a 4 px blur; expanding from the rail, labels fade in. The width still changes at once. **A group slides open and closed** in height (350 ms, `--uix-ease-soft`, from the keyboard too; operator 2026-11-08), its rows arriving 33 ms apart, and its chevron turns over the same 350 ms; the sidebar's own width still changes at once. **A group's rows no longer carry a bullet** (operator, 2026-11-08, as in animate-ui): they start where the parent's label starts (`padding-inline-start` = the row's padding, its icon and the gap).
- **Select and Menu** (animate-ui's pace, operator 2026-11-08): the list grows from `.9` over 220 ms on `--uix-ease-soft`, visible within 100 ms, and shrinks away over the same 220 ms; from the keyboard too. Its rows arrive top to bottom, 33 ms apart, sliding 8 px in from the inline end over 300 ms and clearing from a 4 px blur (`--uix-arrive-blur`, driven per frame in script). A Select picked with the mouse holds its list open 250 ms (`--uix-hold-pick`) while the option's check draws itself (300 ms); a key pick closes at once. The trigger's old value rises out while the new one rises in (400 ms, 4 px blur); the old value is the trigger's `::before` (`data-leaving`), so the trigger's text never holds two values.
- **Tooltip** (animate-ui's tooltip, rebuilt natively): grows from `.7` out of its trigger on `--uix-ease-tooltip` and shrinks back into it on close (it had no exit before). It lingers `--uix-delay-tooltip-skip` (300 ms) after the pointer leaves, so moving to a neighbour carries the same bubble across: it travels there, its background resizing, while the old label slides out (130 ms) and the new one slides in (340 ms), 40 px along the way it travels, both clipped to the background and clearing from a 6 px blur. It is placed in the frame it opens (it was painted for one frame at the viewport's corner before it moved: the "jump"). Escape and blur close it at once. **Markup:** the bubble's content is `.uix-tooltip__content > .uix-tooltip__label`; during a hand-over an `aria-hidden` `.uix-tooltip__content--leaving` copy of the old label sits in the bubble for about 400 ms. `getByRole('tooltip')` text is unchanged at rest. The bubble is `overflow: visible` (the UA's popover `overflow: auto` made it 10 px taller for a frame mid-glide, so it landed too high).
- **Drawer:** side drawers float `--uix-drawer-gap` (8 px) in from the screen edges with `--uix-radius-lg` corners; the bottom sheet stays edge to edge. The panel slides in over 400 ms on `--uix-ease-drawer` (animate-ui's curve: it lands with a 1 % overshoot) and leaves in 160 ms, speeding up as it goes (`--uix-ease-in`, the operator's pick of animate-ui's exit); the dim fades in and out over 400 ms on `--uix-ease-soft` over a constant `--uix-scrim-blur` (the radius never animates; set the token to 0px to turn it off). Touch and pen can drag it toward its edge to close (past a third, or a fling); with `dirty` it settles back and asks first; the mouse keeps close, Escape and the backdrop.
- **Modal:** keeps its calm rise (operator, 2026-11-08); the dim fades in and out over 400 ms on `--uix-ease-soft` over a constant blur of the page behind.
- **Checkbox and Radio** (animate-ui's): the box's fill grows from `.7` over 300 ms (`--uix-ease-fill`), then the tick draws itself 200 ms later (stroke over `pathLength` 1, 300 ms); unticking erases the stroke. The radio dot springs in from nothing on `--uix-ease-bounce` (stiffness 200, damping 16: an 11.6 % overshoot) and out on the same spring.
- **Animated icons:** `animate` on any icon (`<DownloadIcon animate />`), with animate-ui's numbers: hovering its control, an arrow or chevron leans the way it points (25 % / 17 % of the glyph, 300 ms) and eases back when the pointer leaves; close and plus turn a quarter (400 ms); the gear turns half a revolution (1.25 s); the bell swings (0.9 s); refresh, send, download/upload, bin lid, copy sheet (1 px) and star play one short motion; a check draws itself with a small bump (600 ms). Adds `data-icon` and `data-animate` only when set. Hover only; never under reduced motion.
- **Step entrance:** `.uix-anim-step-forward` / `.uix-anim-step-back` for a panel keyed by its step (24 px from the way the person is going over 300 ms on `--uix-ease-enter`, mirrored in RTL).
- **Keyboard:** Select, Menu and a sidebar group now animate from the keyboard too, and the hover highlight follows the arrow keys and Tab (operator, 2026-11-08, as animate-ui); before, a keyboard open was instant. Modal, Drawer, Popover, Peek and the command palette keep their instant keyboard opens.
- **Blurs run in script, never in CSS:** animate-ui clears arriving text from a short blur, and so does UIx now (list rows, the tooltip's labels), with `blurIn` writing the radius each frame while CSS keeps the fade and the slide. A CSS animation or transition of `filter` crashed Linux WebKit's renderer on the first or second list open (measured, in the page and in a popover); the same blur written per frame, as animate-ui's motion library does, ran 40 rounds without a crash, and so did animate-ui's own tooltip. `check-motion` now rejects `filter` and `backdrop-filter` in transitions and `@keyframes`. The value rise-in, the sidebar labels and the step entrance still fade and move without a blur. The blur behind modals and drawers is constant: its radius never animates.
- **Gates:** `check-motion` passes with every new loop/moving rule behind reduced motion; `motion-glide-dom.test.mjs` and `tests/a11y/motion-glide.spec.mjs` (both themes) prove the glide, the tooltip landing, the staggered rows, the floating drawer, the icons and that keyboard and reduced motion get none of it.
