A per-row action panel (enterprise UX study, roadmap #10): `DataWorkspace` `getRowActions` (and `actionPanelTrigger`), `ActionPanel`, `ActionPanelScope`, `groupActionPanelActions`, `ACTION_PANEL_SHORTCUT_ID`, `DEFAULT_ACTION_PANEL_LABELS`. With focus on a row, Enter runs the row's `primary` action (`onRowActivate` stays the primary when set) and Ctrl/⌘+K opens a modal panel anchored to the row: every action with the keys it has now, searchable with the palette's ranking, grouped, destructive actions last (also when searching), confirmed in the panel unless `undoable`. First press local, second press global, through the shortcut registry's scopes: the rows' Ctrl/⌘+K (`uix.action-panel`) outranks the palette's, and inside the open panel it closes it, gives focus back to the row and passes the key on to the `CommandPalette`; with no row focused the palette gets the first press. Each action's `keys` are registered by its `id` in the rows' scope, so they work only with focus in the rows, the `?` sheet lists them under the workspace label, and the single-key switch and remaps apply (WCAG 2.1.4). A ⋯ button per row opens the panel by pointer. Focus returns to the row (or the button) on close. Words: `UixLabelsProvider` `actionPanel`. Its styles are in `components/command-palette.css` (the palette's list, hosted per item) and the row button in `data-workspace.css`. Spec: `Docs/specs/2026-11-20-action-panel.md`.
