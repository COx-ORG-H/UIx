**`SchedulingCalendar` day view by resource** (HAR-983; MEDx S10 truthful day view, S13 slot actions, S48 phone day list). With `view="day"` and `resources` (`{ id, label, description? }`) the day is one column per resource on a slot axis of real instants in `timeZone` (a clock-change day is as long as it really is). `resourceSpans` (`{ id, resourceId, start, end, kind: 'free' | 'booked' | 'off', label?, accessibleName? }`) are drawn time-aligned: a free span is cut into bookable slots of `slotMinutes` (default 15), a booking and time off stay whole, overlapping spans share the width, and a time no span covers is hatched "no schedule" — never free, nothing is inferred from missing data. `dayStart` / `dayEnd` set the axis (default `07:00`–`19:00`). `onSelectSlot` receives `{ resourceId, start, end, kind, span }` for a click, Enter or Space on a slot or a booking; `selectedSlot` marks one (`aria-current`). Keyboard: one tab stop; ArrowUp / ArrowDown within a column, ArrowLeft / ArrowRight to the nearest time in the next column that has something to select, Home / End. The time gutter and the heads stay in view while the grid scrolls inside its own box. `loading` shows skeletons on the first load and keeps the spans on screen, dimmed and `aria-busy`, on a refetch; `emptyNote` when there are no spans. Below `resourceListBelow` (default 480 px of the calendar's own width) the day is a list, one resource after another, with "no schedule" rows for the gaps; `resourceLayout` fixes `columns` or `list`. New words (all optional in `SchedulingCalendarLabels`): `resourceGrid`, `availability`, `noSchedule`, `slotName`, `resourceHint`, `noResources`. The model is exported: `resourceDaySlots`, `layoutResourceColumn`, `resourceDayGaps` and their types.

`scheduling-calendar.css`: the resource day's parts; no kind takes a hue (the band alone does): free is open with a thin bar, a booking filled with a heavy bar and its words, time off quiet words, no schedule hatched; on paper they read the print map.
