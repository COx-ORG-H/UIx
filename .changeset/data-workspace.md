**`DataWorkspace`** (HAR-1388; UIx ADR-0005; TENSOR `data-table.tsx`, MOTUS `UixDataWorkspace.tsx`): the generic presentation half of a data table, composed from the kit's pieces and driven by props. Toolbar in one order: saved view (`savedViews` → `SavedViewMenu`), Save changes / Save as when `viewChanged`, filter chips with a × and "+ Filter" over `FilterEditor` (`filters`), search (`search`, Ctrl/⌘ + / focuses it), View (`viewMenu` → `ViewMenu`), maximize (`maximized` / `onMaximizedChange`, Escape restores), then `toolbarEnd`. A `Table` with the record-id cell (`idColumn`: the id opens a peek, ↗ links to the record), sortable headers (`sort` / `onSortChange`), selection kept by id with Shift-click ranges and select-all (`selection` / `onSelectionChange`), a `BulkBar` (`bulkActions`), a frozen row-actions column (`rowActions`), and `getRowProps` (e.g. for `ChangeHighlight`). Footer: "x–y of N", rows per page and `Pagination` (`pagination`: offset or cursor). States: first-load skeletons (`status="loading"`), empty, filtered empty with Clear filters, error with retry, and a refetch that keeps the rows under `TableWrap refreshing` (`refreshing`). Words from `labels` or `UixLabelsProvider` `dataWorkspace`. The product keeps its surface registry, URL state, fetching, sorting, filtering and permissions. New stylesheet `data-workspace.css`.
